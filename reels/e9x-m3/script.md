## Footage notes

- **Engine audio is the whole video.** Record a real M3 with a separate recorder or lav near the exhaust, with a windscreen. The camera mic will clip at redline. Never rip audio from YouTube.
- **Get the owner's okay** before filming their car or its plates at a meet.
- **330i shots:** Use it only for generic E90 shots (wheel, cabin, road POV). Never show its tach or engine bay as the M3's.
- **Safe zone:** Keep text and the subject out of the bottom fifth and the right-edge button column.
- **Hooks 1 and 3** both open on the tach shot, so one shoot covers both.
